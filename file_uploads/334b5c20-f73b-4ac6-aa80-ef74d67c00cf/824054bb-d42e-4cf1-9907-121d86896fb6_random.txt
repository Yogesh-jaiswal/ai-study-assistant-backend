The city woke slowly beneath a blanket of pale morning fog, with distant sounds of traffic blending into the chatter of early birds. Along the riverbank, joggers moved past rows of trees whose leaves shimmered with dew, while small cafés prepared for the day's first customers. In a nearby library, students gathered around tables scattered with notebooks and laptops, discussing ideas ranging from machine learning to ancient history. As the sun climbed higher, the fog dissolved into a clear blue sky, revealing the bustling streets and vibrant colors of the buildings that had been hidden only moments before.
